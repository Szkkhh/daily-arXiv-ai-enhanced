TL;DR: 提出了一个面向严格黑盒LLM的成员推断方法WPMIA，仅依赖文本输出，通过词级概率估计和前缀条件似然提升攻击效果，并在开源与专有模型上取得了显著性能。

  <summary>Details</summary>

However, most existing MIAs require access to per-token logits or probabilities, making them inapplicable in practice to proprietary LLMs that expose only textual continuations. These results offer a sound foundation for future research on strict black-box membership inference. Code is available at \href{https://github.com/niusj03/WPMIA}{https://github.com/niusj03/WPMIA}.

  LLM-agent evaluation. BenchShield grounds detection in a finite lifecycle model of an evaluation's reward-relevant events. Within the
